About

Actor. Storyteller.
Always training.

Temur Lomtadze is a South Florida-based actor originally from Georgia and previously based in New York.

He began acting at a young age and developed his craft through theatre, film work, scene study, and continued professional training.

His current focus is film and television, with an emphasis on grounded, truthful performances and character-driven storytelling.

Temur continues to train, study technique, and build experience through independent film, scene work, and professional acting opportunities.
