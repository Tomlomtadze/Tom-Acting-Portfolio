About

My journey into acting.

“To Get Something You Never Had, You Have to Do Something You Never Did”

Growing up in Georgia

I was born and raised in Tbilisi, Georgia, and I fell in love with acting when I was nine. I started with small one-scene roles built around physical comedy - playing characters like a mime, clown, and a caveman - before moving into supporting roles such as Aladdin, a magician, a chess pawn and school bully all in "Crazies Theatre."

Rustaveli Theatre

At Rustaveli Theatre, often known as the "Theatre of Stars," I landed my first lead role in The Adventures of the Wooden Boy, a Pinocchio-inspired production. It became an important milestone in my early acting journey.

Moving to New York

In 2019, I moved to New York. During Covid-19, I found training acting opportunities often in smaller outdoor settings, I was struggling to find where I fit. At the same time, I was adjusting to a completely new culture and trying to understand my place in the U.S.

Re-discovery

It all changed when I joined Advanced Theatre at James Madison High School. Over the next three years, I grew through constant scene work, performances, and training, eventually the program's theatre medal at graduation. Outside of school, I also perfomed with Dancing Crane, taking part in Georgian productions staged at different theatres around New York.

Film & Rejection

After high school, I continued pursuing acting in college, but I also faced rejection that forced me to rethink where I was headed. I began searching for opportunities and eventually landed my first short film.

Moving to Miami

After moving to South Florida, I started rebuilding my acting life in a new city - looking for stronger training, more film opportunties and a clearer path. I began training in Miami Acting Studio working on scenes, self-tapes, and material I could actually use professionally. I'm focused on presenting myself more seriously as an actor.
