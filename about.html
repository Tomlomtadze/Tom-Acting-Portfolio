About

My journey into acting.

“To Get Something You Never Had, You Have to Do Something You Never Did”

Growing up in Georgia

I was born and raised in Tbilisi, Georgia, and I fell in love with acting when I was nine. I started with small one-scene roles built around physical comedy - playing characters like a mime, clown, and ancient cannibal - before moving into supporting roles such as Aladdin, a magician, a chess pawn and school bully all in "Crazies Theatre."

Rustaveli Theatre

At Rustaveli Theatre, often known as the "Theatre of Stars," I landed my first lead role in The Adventures of the Wooden Boy, a Pinocchio-inspired production. It became an important milestone in my early acting journey.

Moving to New York

In 2019, I moved to New York. During Covid-19, I found training acting opportunities often in smaller outdoor settings, I was struggling to find where I fit. At the same time, I was adjusting to a completely new culture and trying to understand my place in the U.S.
